CAPABILITIES AND ACTUAL BOUNDARIES
1. Context-aware emotion: local, tentative text cues guide calmer or warmer
   responses. Existing serious-context guidance survives scheduling follow-ups;
   explicit resolution/positive news can change delivery. No diagnostic emotion
   inference, acoustic emotion classifier or guaranteed vocal performance.
2. Smart conversation memory: existing structured/confirmed state plus at most
   six caller context anchors and four recent excerpts outside rolling history.
   This is current-call memory. Existing explicit memory tools/history continue
   to govern cross-call storage; no automatic customer profile scraping.
3. Intelligent call recovery: native provider SDK retries/resumption during the
   same call, with at most two recoverable-error allowances. Permanent or
   exhausted failures close safely. No automatic redial, provider switching,
   recovery of a terminated SIP call, or guaranteed worker-crash restoration.
   Existing CallTasks continue settling submitted writes without replay.
4. Quality intelligence: counts of observed repair/repetition/tool/provider
   issues and up to 64 transcript-associated SDK speech-end -> room-playback
   samples. Reports label p95, sample coverage and measurement boundaries.
   No fabricated quality score, semantic accuracy score or handset timing.
5. Self-correction: challenged replies trigger focused repair guidance without
   discarding confirmed details. Invalid native tool arguments return an
   explicit no-submission result so the model can correct its arguments rather
   than asking the caller again. Unsupported booking claims are flagged;
   end_call(booked) cannot succeed without a booking receipt. This does not
   retroactively retract speech already heard or guarantee no hallucinations.
6. Business outcomes: booking, current-recipient email and human transfer use
   existing tool receipts. Queued/sending/sent/failed are distinct. Spoken claims
   alone cannot make a real call booked; verified results inform existing call
   outcome/was_booked fields. Existing booking/email/transfer implementations
   and campaign analytics remain in place. A confirmed existing booking is not
   proof that a NEW appointment was created in this call.

REPORTS
Calls -> open a completed new call -> Conversation intelligence.
Existing calls without a collected report show a clear not-recorded state;
they are not retrospectively assigned fictional metrics.
Reports merge into Call.metadata.callIntelligence, preserving dispatch/provider
metadata. They contain bounded counts/statuses, not names, email addresses,
caller excerpts, booking IDs or provider session handles.
Persistence is best effort after the call, with a three-second timeout; failure
does not break the completed call. The existing protected call page displays it.
Browser demo remains sandbox-only and writes its report to its local journal;
it does not create a real booking, email or transfer to populate the UI.
